About

CampusVote is an online voting system built for SNSU student organizations and classrooms. It replaces manual, paper-based elections with a faster, more reliable process, from candidate registration to real-time result tallying. Every enrolled student can vote securely from any device, without the long lines, printed ballots, or manual counting that slow down traditional campus elections. Voting runs within a set schedule, from the opening to the closing of the polls, so students can cast their vote anytime within that window, even in between classes. CampusVote makes student-led elections easier to run, easier to trust, and easier to take part in.
